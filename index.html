N° 01 — The House

Birds of a feather
flock together.

The Lucky Goose is a room on the Ta' Xbiex seafront that does a few things properly: tapas, pasta rolled fresh each day, and a cabinet of steaks aged in-house — chosen by eye, carved to order.

The menu follows the market and the board changes daily — a glass at the bar to start, the marina across the road, the lamps low.
